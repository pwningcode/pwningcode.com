## /work page — FVC case-study bridge (not published yet)

A `/work` page with two or three anonymized FVC case studies. Lives on
pwningcode (the practitioner site), not fvcsolutions.com (the sales site).
FVC's portfolio page can link to it. Reads as practitioner showing work,
not as marketing.

### Case-study format

For each engagement, write a short section using this shape:

- **The problem.** What the client was actually paying for or drowning in
  before we got involved.
- **What we built.** Concrete. "PDF intake plus LLM extraction plus
  Postgres plus a 4-route admin UI." Not "an AI-powered solution."
- **The hard part.** The thing that wasn't obvious going in. The
  failure mode we hit, the assumption that broke.
- **What it cost / how long.** Match the numbers FVC already lists on
  its services pages ($5k discovery, $15k-$30k build, 3-8 weeks).
- **What we'd do differently.** The honest postscript.

### Open questions before building this

- Anonymization sign-off from clients. Some will say yes, some will say
  no. If no one says yes, fall back to an archetype-driven page
  ("the kind of thing we build looks like this") with composite examples.
- Which engagements to feature. Probably want one of each shape:
  internal tool, document intelligence, customer-facing AI. Maybe a
  process-automation one too.
- Whether to include client logos at all. Probably not. The story is
  the work, not the logo wall.

### When to actually build it

After two or three FVC engagements have been delivered with case-study
permission, and after the regular /notes posts have established the
practitioner voice. Premature otherwise.
